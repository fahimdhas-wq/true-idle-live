# ⚔️ True IDLE — Playable Build

**▶ PLAY NOW:** https://fahimdhas-wq.github.io/true-idle-live/

True IDLE is a persistent, browser-based idle RPG. Progress is saved
automatically in your browser (localStorage) — no account, no server, no data
collection. Just open the link and play.

## How to play
1. Open the link above. The game loads and starts fighting on Floor 1.
2. Combat is automatic. Your character kills monsters, gains XP, levels up,
   and dives deeper into dungeons on its own.
3. Spend resources on:
   - **Enchanting** — upgrade your weapon & armour with rare drops.
   - **Farming / Mining / Herblore** — gather materials and brew potions.
   - **Slayer / Beasts / Necromancy** — unlock deeper systems & summons.
   - **Sanctified Arts / Promotions / Achievements** — long-term power.
4. Use the tab bar (or ☰ menu on mobile) to switch systems.
5. Your progress is saved in this browser. Use **Save** in Settings to force a
   save, or **Wipe** to reset.

## Notes
- Fully client-side: runs entirely in your browser.
- Best on desktop or mobile (responsive layout).
- Source code lives in a **private** repository: the link above is the only
  public artifact (the compiled game).
